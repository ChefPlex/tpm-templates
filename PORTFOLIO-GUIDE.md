# I need to…

A reading path across the whole ChefPlex TPM portfolio, by the situation you are in. Each path is short and in order: read the first item first. Everything here is free to use; see each repository's license.

The portfolio is eight repositories. You do not need to know which one holds what. Start from your situation below.

---

## By situation

### I'm new to program management

1. [TPM Craft: Notes on Running Programs at Scale](https://github.com/ChefPlex/learning-notes/blob/main/tpm-craft-notes.md)
2. [Program Phases Playbook](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-templates/blob/main/program-phases-playbook.md): what happens in each phase, and what "done" means for it
3. [The RAID Log: What It Is, Why It Matters, and How to Use It](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-templates/blob/main/raid-log-guide.md)
4. [Stakeholder Engagement Playbook](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-templates/blob/main/stakeholder-engagement-playbook.md)
5. Coming from another career? [Introducing PM Concepts to Career Changers](https://github.com/ChefPlex/learning-notes/blob/main/pm-for-career-changers.md)

### I'm kicking off a program

1. [Program Kickoff Checklist](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-toolbox/blob/main/program-kickoff-checklist.md)
2. [Program Charter Template](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-templates/blob/main/program-charter-template.md), with a [sample excerpt](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-templates/blob/main/examples/sample-program-charter-excerpt.md)
3. RACI, [agile](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-templates/blob/main/raci-template-agile.md) or [waterfall](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-templates/blob/main/raci-template-waterfall.md)
4. [Communications Plan Template](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-templates/blob/main/communications-plan-template.md)
5. [RAID Log Template](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-toolbox/blob/main/raid-log-template.md) (also as an [Excel workbook](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-toolbox/blob/main/raid-log-template.xlsx))

### I'm taking over a program that is off track

1. [Rescuing Work That Is Off Track](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-templates/blob/main/rescuing-work-that-is-off-track.md)
2. [The RAID Log guide](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-templates/blob/main/raid-log-guide.md): rebuild the real one first
3. [Program Status Reporting Framework](https://github.com/ChefPlex/program-reporting-frameworks/blob/main/program-status-reporting-framework.md): reset what "Yellow" means
4. [Steering Committee Deck Structure](https://github.com/ChefPlex/program-reporting-frameworks/blob/main/steering-committee-deck-structure.md): get the decisions you need
5. A second pair of eyes: the [What Am I Missing prompt](https://github.com/ChefPlex/ai-automations/blob/main/prompts/director-review/what-am-i-missing.md)

### I need a status report by Friday

1. [Program Status Reporting Framework](https://github.com/ChefPlex/program-reporting-frameworks/blob/main/program-status-reporting-framework.md), with a [sample Red / Yellow / Green status](https://github.com/ChefPlex/program-reporting-frameworks/blob/main/examples/sample-red-yellow-green-status.md)
2. Drafting from your notes with AI: the [Weekly Status Workflow](https://github.com/ChefPlex/ai-automations/blob/main/workflows/weekly-status-workflow.md) and the [Status Report Generator](https://github.com/ChefPlex/ai-automations/tree/main/status-report-generator)
3. Drafting from Slack: [Slack Canvas Status Synthesis](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-toolbox/tree/main/slack-canvas-status-synthesis)
4. Before you send anything AI-drafted: the [Status Contract](https://github.com/ChefPlex/tpm-toolbox/blob/main/STATUS-CONTRACT.md), what a draft may and may not decide

### I need a decision or money from leadership
